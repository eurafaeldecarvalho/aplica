Applies to jobs in your own browser

A Claude Code plugin and desktop app that finds roles, scores them against your target and fills the applications with your real data, at a human pace. Anything risky it leaves for you.

Download Aplica View on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT). Windows and Linux. Needs a paid Claude plan and the Claude in Chrome extension.

Set it up once, then let it work

Your data, typed once

Name, contact, work authorization and the answers forms keep asking for. Aplica fills applications only from this profile, so nothing on a form is invented. It stays in a folder on your computer.

In the clip: a sample profile filled in, then work authorization below it.

Say what you're after

Titles to search, the stack you want, the stacks that rule a job out, the markets you accept and the ones you don't. Every role found is scored against this before it gets a slot.

In the clip: "Platform Engineer" added as a search term, then the stack and market filters.

Decide how hard it pushes

Applications per day, batch size, seconds between actions and the hours it may work. Comments, connections and DMs, the biggest ban triggers, start switched off.

In the clip: 15 applications a day, 45 to 240 seconds between actions.

It tells you where it stopped

A blocked site, an expired sign-in, a form that wants a document upload: each lands in Notifications with what to do next, beside the result of the last run.

In the clip: the dashboard, then Notifications with one block and one task for you.

What it never does

These are part of the plugin's rules. When a form needs one of them, the application goes to your "needs you" list to finish by hand.

  1. Pay for anythingIt never enters card or bank details.
  2. Upload your documentsIDs, diplomas and other personal files stay with you.
  3. Make up an answerForms, cover letters and follow-ups use your profile and the role's recorded facts only.
  4. Change a password or accept an offerAccount and career decisions are yours.
  5. Push through a CAPTCHA or a blockIt stops at once and tells you.

Before you start

Their terms restrict automated access, and any automation can get an account limited. That is why social actions start off, caps are low, it uses your own account and IP at a human pace, and it stops on any CAPTCHA or block. Read DISCLAIMER.md and decide for yourself.

Your profile, answers, résumés and the application database stay in your own config folder, never in the repo. What the agent reads to fill a form goes to Claude like any Claude Code session.

A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), the Claude in Chrome extension with your accounts signed in, and Python 3.11 or later. The desktop app checks each one and installs the plugin for you.

Yes. The target is plain config: titles, skills, locations, work modes and salary floor. The shipped example is a backend template; replace it with yours.

Aplica is free and MIT-licensed. It runs on the Claude plan you already pay for.

Start your first round

Install the app, fill in your profile once and press Run. Prefer the terminal? In Claude Code:

/plugin install aplica --marketplace eurafaeldecarvalho/aplica
