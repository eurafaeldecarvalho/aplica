# Disclaimer — read before you run Aplica

Aplica automates **your own** job search, on **your own** computer, in **your own**
browser sessions, with **your own** real data. It is the digital equivalent of you
sitting down and filling in job applications yourself — the automation changes the
speed, not the nature of the act. You are the only person responsible for how you use it.

By using Aplica you acknowledge:

1. **Job boards may prohibit automation.** LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and others
   restrict automated access in their Terms of Service. Using any automation —
   including this one — can lead to your account being **rate-limited, restricted or
   permanently banned**. This is a civil (contract) matter, not a criminal one, but the
   consequence is real and falls on **your** account. Social actions (commenting,
   connecting, DMing) and high volume are the biggest triggers, which is why they are
   **OFF by default** here.

2. **Use your own single account and your own IP.** Never run Aplica against accounts
   that are not yours, never use multiple accounts, and never chain it with other
   auto-apply tools on the same account.

3. **Honesty is built in and must stay.** Aplica never invents or inflates facts and
   never submits data that is not true. Keep it that way — a false fact burns you in the
   interview and can be fraud.

4. **No warranty.** Aplica is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind (see
   `LICENSE`). The authors are not liable for banned accounts, missed opportunities,
   wrong submissions, or any other outcome.

5. **You stay in control of risky steps.** Aplica never pays, never enters card or bank
   details, never uploads personal documents (ID/passport), never changes passwords or
   security settings, and never accepts offers. Anything like that is logged for **you**
   to finish by hand.

If you are not comfortable with the account-ban risk, keep the social features off, keep
the daily caps low, or do not use automation at all. The conservative defaults exist for
exactly this reason.
